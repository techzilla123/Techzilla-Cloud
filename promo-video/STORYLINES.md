# Storylines

Assumption: 39 seconds, 16:9 desktop video, spoken narration, and the project's
existing sample music bed; screen captures use a 1400 × 788 desktop viewport.

## Recommended — The broken screen becomes a bigger screen

**Device and form:** One object-to-object match cut: a cracked tablet gives way
to the real Techzilla Cloud browser, then its music area opens into the full
music page. The viewer starts with a familiar frustration and sees the same
need answered by the actual website.

**First two seconds:** “2 DAYS AGO” lands beside the close-up of a worn tablet;
the words “I got access to my old tablet again” begin as its screen lights up.

**Beats:** old tablet → cracked, difficult screen → web developer builds
Techzilla Cloud → files across devices → landing-page song click → music-page
quick-pick song click → Techzilla Cloud.

**Look:** The site's own warm-white ground, deep teal ink and editorial
Playfair/Inter type; a simple glass-and-metal tablet illustration leads into
unmodified desktop captures of the product.

**Sound:** A restrained, low-volume sample bed under a plainspoken narration;
one soft click on the demo handoff and a short whoosh on the reveal.

## The track is the thread

**Device and form:** An endless zoom follows a song row from the landing page
into the Music page, then pulls back to reveal the larger library. The old
tablet is a brief interruption that explains why a reliable, larger workspace
matters.

**First two seconds:** The actual Recently played section fills the screen as
the narrator says, “Two days ago, I got access to my old tablet again.”

**Beats:** song row → broken tablet → Techzilla Cloud reveal → landing-page
music → Music page → closing name.

**Look:** Actual product screens, deep teal and warm white; close crops expand
into full desktop views.

**Sound:** A restrained sample bed under narration, with a soft click at the
real navigation handoff.

## One place for every device

**Device and form:** Three real desktop views travel side by side, then
collapse into the actual Music page. The broken tablet is the reason to stop
working around old hardware and gather the library in one place.

**First two seconds:** A full-width desktop capture of the landing page cuts
to the cracked-tablet illustration on “the screen was broken.”

**Beats:** broken screen → web developer's idea → Techzilla Cloud → landing
page → Music page → one place for the library.

**Look:** Real site captures on the site's warm-white and deep-teal palette;
the side-by-side layout is used only to compare real views.

**Sound:** A restrained sample bed and first-person narration; no effects
between the two real website views.
