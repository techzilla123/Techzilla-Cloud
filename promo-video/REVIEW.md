## Creative and capture notes

The short first-person story moves from recovering an old tablet with a broken
screen to building Techzilla Cloud, then demonstrates the real landing-page
music section and Music page. The product captures use a 1400 × 788 desktop
viewport and fill the final frame. A visible cursor moves to and clicks songs
in both captures. Music-page playback was verified active in the capture; the
player progress and pause control are visible. The composition uses the site's
brand mark and color palette; the website UI itself was not changed for this
video.

Narration was generated locally and mixed with the project's sample music bed
and soft transition effects. The captured song streams are not used as the
soundtrack; the sample bed is not a recording of the user's Dropbox tracks.
